hello-kubernetes is a small web app to deploy on a Kubernetes cluster. Its page shows:

- a message, **Hello world!** by default
- the namespace, pod and node that answered
- the container image and its architecture

Refresh the page, and another pod can answer. That makes it a simple way to show how a Service spreads requests across pods and nodes, to test an Ingress or a Gateway, or to check a new cluster.

The image runs on `linux/amd64` and `linux/arm64` nodes, as a non-root user. It is published to Docker Hub as `paulbouwer/hello-kubernetes` and to GHCR as `ghcr.io/paulbouwer/hello-kubernetes`, and the Helm chart is at `oci://ghcr.io/paulbouwer/charts/hello-kubernetes`.

Start with the [quick start](/hello-kubernetes/docs/start-here/quick-start/).
